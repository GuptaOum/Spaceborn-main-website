## 🎨 Design Rules & Standard
1. **Obsidian Palette:** True blacks (`#000000`, `#050505`), card surfaces (`#0C0D0E`), hairline borders (`border-white/10`). Eliminate noisy neon greens and electric cyans.
2. **Typography:** Retire decorative display fonts (`Ethnocentric`). Use clean geometric sans (`Inter` / `Geist`) for display and `IBM Plex Mono` for technical telemetry tags, telemetry badges (`[SYS_ACTIVE]`), and coordinates.
3. **Hard Engineering Specifications:** Replace vague marketing copy with concrete figures (TOPS/W compute, latency in ms, payload capacity, sensor resolution, flight range).
4. **Fluid Spring Physics:** Use Framer Motion spring dynamics (`stiffness: 100, damping: 20`) for tactile, weighted interactions.
5. **High-Performance 3D & Media:** Three.js scenes must disconnect/pause via `IntersectionObserver` when scrolled offscreen.
